Rounds for iPhone and Apple Watch

Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

The short version

Rounds has no account, no advertising, and no tracking. Your workouts and history stay on your devices. Product analytics is off unless you choose to share it, and you can turn it off and delete it at any time. If you send feedback, only the text you write is sent. Rounds never reads or transmits your Apple Health data.

What stays on your devices

Rounds runs on iPhone and Apple Watch. Your workouts, your workout history, your preferences, and any username you choose are stored on your iPhone and, for workouts that run on the Watch, your Apple Watch. The two devices sync with each other. There is no account, password, or sign-in, and Rounds does not ask for your name or email address.

Product analytics (optional)

The first time you open Rounds, it asks whether to share product analytics. Choosing “Continue without analytics” sends nothing, and the app works the same either way. Nothing is collected before you opt in.

If you opt in, Rounds records and sends these kinds of events:

  • Which app screens you open, such as Discover, My Workouts, the workout builder, and Settings.
  • Exercise filters and which catalog exercises you pick.
  • Creating, editing, deleting, importing, and sharing your workouts, with the workout type (AMRAP, EMOM, or straight sets).
  • Viewing, saving, and sharing workouts in the public library, and where in the app they appeared.
  • Workout sessions: start, pause, resume, set or round counts, and finish or interruption, including the workout type, elapsed time, and the number of sets and rounds you recorded. These come from the Rounds timer, not from Apple Health.
  • Opening or sending a feedback form (the fact that you did so, never its text).

Analytics never includes Apple Health data, your username, search text, feedback text, or names you give to custom exercises.

Events are linked to a random installation ID, not to your name or Apple ID. The ID is created on your iPhone using Apple’s App Attest service, is specific to Rounds, and cannot be used to identify you in other apps. It is not the advertising identifier. Events are saved on your devices and sent to our servers when you are connected. Apple Watch events are passed through your iPhone.

Analytics data is processed and stored by Supabase, our hosting provider, acting for us.

How long analytics data is kept

  • Raw events: up to 365 days.
  • Daily summaries that still carry your installation ID: 13 months.
  • Monthly summaries, which contain only counts and no installation IDs: 5 years.

Withdrawing and deleting

Open Account (the person button at the top of Discover and Library) and choose “Turn off and delete analytics data.” Rounds stops collecting, clears events waiting on your devices, and asks our servers to delete the data linked to your installation. If you are offline, the deletion is queued and completes when you reconnect. Monthly summaries are anonymous totals that cannot be traced back to you, so they remain until they expire. You can opt in again later.

Feature ideas, bug reports, and exercise requests

Rounds lets you send a feature idea, a bug report, or a request for a new exercise. We receive only the text you type and the category or equipment you pick, along with a randomly generated request ID. Please do not include personal information in these messages. Rounds does not collect your email address and cannot reply to you, so these requests are one-way.

Request text is stored in our feedback database, hosted by Supabase, for up to 180 days. A request is copied into the developer’s private issue tracker on GitHub so it can be tracked, and it is reviewed with the help of AI: the text is sent to Anthropic’s Claude to summarize and sort it. A person reviews any resulting change. You can withdraw a feature idea or bug report from within the app, which deletes it from the feedback database.

To limit abuse, our servers apply rate limits. For this, your IP address is converted into a salted hash and only the hash is used. The Rounds backend does not store raw IP addresses. Our hosting providers may keep standard connection logs under their own policies.

Apple Health

With your permission, the Rounds Watch app uses Apple HealthKit to run a workout session and save your completed workout to Apple Health. Rounds asks for permission to write workouts and does not read your existing Health data. Rounds does not transmit Health data to us or anyone else. Apple Health manages saved workouts under your device and iCloud settings.

You can manage Rounds’ Health permission in the Health app and delete a saved workout from Apple Health at any time.

No advertising, tracking, or sale of data

Rounds contains no advertising and no third-party analytics, advertising, or social networking SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites, does not build advertising profiles, and does not sell or rent your data. The only service providers that handle data are Supabase (hosting), and, for feedback you choose to send, GitHub (issue tracking) and Anthropic (AI-assisted review).

Deleting your information

Deleting a workout removes it from Rounds on your devices. Removing Rounds from your iPhone and Apple Watch removes the information stored by the app. Analytics can be deleted as described above. Workouts already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in Apple Health.

Children

Rounds is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and data requests

You can turn analytics on or off and delete analytics data in the app at any time. For any other question or request about your data, including access or deletion of a feedback submission, contact us below. Because Rounds has no account and does not collect your name or email, we may ask for details such as the approximate date and text of a submission to find it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if Rounds changes. The latest version and its effective date will always be available on this page.
