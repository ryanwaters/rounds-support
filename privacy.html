Rounds for Apple Watch

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 29, 2026

The short version

Rounds uses an anonymous installation ID for community features and moderation. You can optionally enable analytics. Exercise and feature requests may include an email address if you choose to provide one. We do not collect your personal information or Health data. There are no accounts, advertising, or tracking.

Information handled by Rounds

Rounds handles the workout duration you choose, completed round count and round timestamps, workout timing, and your most recently selected duration. This information is used only to run the workout, recover an interrupted session, and keep recent completed results on your device.

Installation identity

Rounds creates an anonymous installation ID (DeviceID) for your installation, registered through Apple App Attest. This identifier supports community features, moderation, and per-installation action counts. It does not identify you personally and is not linked to an account.

The installation ID is created automatically and is required for the app to function.

Optional analytics

You can choose to enable analytics in Rounds settings. When enabled, Rounds collects:

  • Screen views and exercise selections
  • Routine operations (creating, editing, deleting routines)
  • Workout session timing and completion

Analytics does not collect free-text input, personal information, or HealthKit values. Analytics is off by default. You can enable or disable it at any time in settings.

Raw analytics events linked to your installation ID are kept for 365 days. Aggregated statistics are formed only from groups of 20 or more installations and may be kept for up to 5 years. If you disable analytics, identifiable event data is removed, but sealed aggregates are retained.

Exercise and feature requests

When you submit an exercise or feature request through the app, Rounds sends the request content to our server. You may optionally provide an email address for a reply by checking the consent checkbox.

If you provide an email address, we use it only to respond to your request and do not use it for marketing or share it with third parties.

Submitted requests are kept for 30 days, or until the request is closed out, whichever comes first.

Where your information is stored

Workout state, preferences, and recent results are stored locally on your Apple Watch.

Installation identity, analytics events (if enabled), and exercise or feature requests are transmitted to and stored on our server, hosted by Supabase. Supabase is the only third-party data processor used by Rounds.

Apple Health

With your permission, Rounds uses Apple HealthKit to run a live indoor cross-training session and save the completed workout to Apple Health. Rounds requests permission to write workouts and does not request read access to your Health data. Apple Health manages the saved workout and any related workout metrics under your device and iCloud settings.

We do not receive, sell, use for advertising, or share your Health data. You can manage Rounds’ Health permission in the Health app and delete a saved workout from Apple Health at any time.

Third parties and tracking

Rounds contains no third-party advertising or tracking software. It does not create a user profile or use information to track you across apps or websites.

Rounds uses Supabase to host our backend server. Analytics data and exercise or feature requests are processed by Supabase in accordance with their terms.

Deleting information

Choose Discard Workout before saving to discard the active workout. Removing Rounds from your Apple Watch removes information stored locally by the app. Workouts already saved to Apple Health remain there until you delete them through Apple Health.

Disabling analytics in settings removes identifiable analytics event data from our server, though sealed aggregate statistics remain. Your installation ID and any submitted exercise or feature requests remain on our server after removing the app.

You can request deletion of your data by emailing support@sweatfocus.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if Rounds changes. The latest version and its effective date will always be available on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy or Rounds can be sent to support@sweatfocus.com.
