Rounds for Apple Watch

Privacy Policy

Last updated August 13, 2026

The short version

Rounds has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking. Workout, routine, and Health information stays on your devices. If you choose to request an exercise or contact support, we receive the message you submit and any reply address you voluntarily provide.

Information handled by Rounds

Rounds handles the routines you create, workout duration you choose, completed round count and round timestamps, workout timing, and app preferences. This information is used only to build and synchronize routines, run workouts, recover an interrupted session, and keep recent completed results on your devices.

Where your information is stored

Routine data is stored locally on your iPhone and paired Apple Watch. Workout state, preferences, and recent results are stored locally on your Apple Watch. Rounds does not transmit this device-stored workout or routine information to us.

Exercise requests and support

If you use Request an Exercise or contact support, we receive the exercise name and details or support message you submit. You may also choose to provide an email address so we can reply. Submitting a request is optional, and Rounds does not include your workouts, routines, Apple Health information, or a persistent device identifier with it.

We use this information only to receive, secure, review, deduplicate, respond to, and act on requests; prevent spam and abuse; and maintain the integrity of the request process. A request is reviewed before it can enter any exercise-production workflow. We do not use request data for advertising or sell it.

Service providers may process submissions on our behalf for hosting, email delivery, security scanning, and storage. They may process the information only to provide those services to us. Request content and correspondence are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for these purposes and applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Apple Health

With your permission, Rounds uses Apple HealthKit to run a live indoor cross-training session and save the completed workout to Apple Health. Rounds requests permission to write workouts and does not request read access to your Health data. Apple Health manages the saved workout and any related workout metrics under your device and iCloud settings.

We do not receive, sell, use for advertising, or share your Health data. You can manage Rounds’ Health permission in the Health app and delete a saved workout from Apple Health at any time.

Third parties and tracking

Rounds contains no third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or social networking software. It does not create an advertising profile or use information to track you across apps or websites. Providers that operate the optional request and support process are used only for app functionality and security.

Deleting information

Choose Discard Workout before saving to discard the active workout. Removing Rounds from your Apple Watch removes information stored by the app. Workouts already saved to Apple Health remain there until you delete them through Apple Health. You may ask us to delete an exercise request, support correspondence, or optional reply address by contacting us. We may retain limited records when necessary for security, legal compliance, or to honor your deletion request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if Rounds changes. The latest version and its effective date will always be available on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy or Rounds can be sent to support@sweatfocus.com.
