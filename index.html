Quick answers

The essentials.

How do I add or remove a round?

During a running workout, swipe down across the main workout display to add one round. Swipe up to remove one. Short or slightly diagonal swipes work as long as the movement is clearly vertical. You can also tap the + button to add a round.

How do I pause the workout?

Tap Pause at the bottom of the workout screen. The timer and round counting stop until you tap Resume.

How do I end a workout early?

Tap the red stop button, then choose End & Save. Choose Cancel to keep going, or Discard Workout to end without saving it.

Why does Rounds ask for Apple Health access?

Rounds asks for permission to save completed workouts to Apple Health. It does not request permission to read your Health data.

A swipe isn’t registering. What should I try?

Make sure the workout is running rather than paused. Start the gesture anywhere on the main timer and round display, then move clearly toward the bottom or top of the screen.

Can I request an exercise for the catalog?

Yes. In the iPhone app, open Add Exercise and choose Request an Exercise. You can also email an exercise request. Include the exercise name, equipment, and exact variation. Requests are screened and reviewed before any exercise-production work begins.
